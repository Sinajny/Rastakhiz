Saeideh Elham

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Saeideh Elham, 48, killed in Mashhad

On 19 Dey, Saeideh was shot from behind with live ammunition by government forces at Faramarz Intersection in Mashhad and lost her life.

Saeideh was a teacher and mother of four children. She was also a rowing coach, a triathlon referee and a karate practitioner.

Dear Saeideh, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
