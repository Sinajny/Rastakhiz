Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Samad Ahmadi Barehsari

Samad Ahmadi Barehsari, 40, killed in Rasht

On 18 Dey, during the Rasht protests, Samad was shot in the head with live ammunition by government forces and lost his life.

Samad was a laborer, was married and had two children. He was from the village of Barehsar in Rudbar.

He was buried in his hometown of Barehsar on 21 Dey.

Dear Samad, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
