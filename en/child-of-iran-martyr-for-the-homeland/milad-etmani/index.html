Milad Atmani

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Milad Atmani, killed in Punak, Tehran

On 18 Dey, during the protests in Punak, Tehran, Milad went to help his friend Diyar Pourchehriq. He was shot in the hand and then detained. Twelve days later, his family found his body at the Kahrizak morgue, with evidence of two additional gunshot wounds to his head and abdomen.

Milad was from Salmas.

Dear Milad, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
