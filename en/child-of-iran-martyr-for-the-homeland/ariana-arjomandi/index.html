Ariana Arjmandi

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Ariana Arjmandi, 23, killed in Borujerd

On 18 Dey, Ariana went to the protests in Borujerd with her friends. She entered Takhti Street from Shohada Square and, in a side alley, was shot in the heart at close range with pellets by a government agent. She lost her life.

Ariana studied psychology, was learning German and planned to pursue further education.

Her family refused to identify her as a Basij member. She was buried at Borujerd cemetery in the early hours of 22 Dey.

Dear Ariana, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
