Ata Ebrahimpour

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Ata Ebrahimpour, 42, killed in Narmak

On 18 Dey in Narmak, Tehran, Ata was shot by government forces and lost his life. Four live rounds struck his heart, shoulder, lower leg and wrist. His family tried to get him to a hospital, but Ata did not survive.

Ata owned a toy shop in Andisheh Shopping Center, was married and had two young daughters.

He was buried in Section 203 of Behesht-e Zahra Cemetery in Tehran.

Dear Ata, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
