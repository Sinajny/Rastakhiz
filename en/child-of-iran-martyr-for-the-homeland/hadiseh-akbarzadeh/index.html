Hadiseh Akbarzadeh

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Hadiseh Akbarzadeh, 19, killed in Fardis

On 18 Dey, just five days before what the account describes as her nineteenth birthday, Hadiseh was shot from behind by government forces in Fardis. The bullet passed through her chest and she died.
