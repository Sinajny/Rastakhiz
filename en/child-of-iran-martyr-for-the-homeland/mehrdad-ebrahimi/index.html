Mehrdad Ebrahimi

Mehrdad Ebrahimi, 21, killed in Shahroud

On 19 Dey, Mehrdad was at the protests in Shahroud with his brother. When shooting began, before he could take shelter behind a car, government forces struck him in the side with live ammunition. His brother took him to Imam Hossein Hospital in Shahroud, but according to the account, he neither received the necessary care nor was allowed to be transferred to another hospital. Mehrdad died several hours later.

Mehrdad was a civil engineering student.

He was buried in Dizaj Cemetery in Shahroud.
