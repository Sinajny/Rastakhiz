Reza Asadi

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Reza Asadi, 30, killed in Gorgan

On 18 Dey, government forces shot Reza with live ammunition during the protests in Gorgan, killing him.

At his farewell ceremony, Reza's father held up a photograph of his son and said: “People, Reza has been taken from us; he sacrificed himself for the homeland.”
