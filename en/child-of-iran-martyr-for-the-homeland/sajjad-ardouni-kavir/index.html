Sajjad Ardouni Kavir

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Sajjad Ardouni Kavir, 20, killed in Sabzevar

On 18 Dey, Sajjad was shot directly by government forces in the Motahhari area of Sabzevar and lost his life.

Sajjad held a high-school diploma and worked as an auto-body repairer. He dreamed of opening his own repair shop and had even chosen its name: “Miracle.” Despite his young age, he carried much of his household's responsibilities and worked hard for his family.

Dear Sajjad, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
