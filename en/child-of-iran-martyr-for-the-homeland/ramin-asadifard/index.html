Ramin Asadifard

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Ramin Asadifard, 41, killed in the Mesbah neighborhood of Karaj

On 18 Dey, Ramin was at Mesbah Intersection in Karaj and took several wounded people to a clinic. There, government forces shot him with live ammunition and he lost his life.

He was buried at Golzar-e Shohada cemetery in Kalak-e Bala, Karaj.

Dear Ramin, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
