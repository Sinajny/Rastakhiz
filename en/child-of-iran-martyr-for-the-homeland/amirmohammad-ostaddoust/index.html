Amirmohammad Ostaddoust

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Amirmohammad Ostaddoust, 23, killed in Ramsar

On 18 Dey, during the protests in Ramsar, government forces shot Amirmohammad with live ammunition and he lost his life.

During the farewell ceremony at his grave, his family and loved ones held a traditional henna ceremony.
