Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Amirhossein Emamjomeh

Amirhossein Emamjomeh, 39, killed in Haft-Hoz, Narmak, Tehran

On 18 Dey, Amirhossein was attending the protests in Haft-Hoz, Narmak, with his wife when government forces fired from the roof of a mosque and shot him in the face with live ammunition. He died in his wife's arms.

Amirhossein worked for a pharmaceutical and food distribution company and was the father of a young daughter.

His body was handed over to his family at Kahrizak. They were asked to present him as an officially recognized government 'martyr', but refused. The bullet remained lodged in his face, and he was buried with it.
