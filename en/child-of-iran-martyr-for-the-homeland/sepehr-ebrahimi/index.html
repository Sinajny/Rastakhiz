Sepehr Ebrahimi

Child of Iran, Martyr for the Homeland
Sepehr Ebrahimi, 19, died in Andisheh Township, Tehran.

On 19 Dey, Sepehr left home. His mother feared he might join the protests and be harmed. To reassure her, he said he was going to the gym, but he joined the protesters. His family heard nothing from him for seven days, until his body was found. According to the Persian memorial account, government forces shot and killed him.

Sepehr was a boxer and devoted much of his life to training and sports.

He was buried at Behesht-e Zahra cemetery in Tehran.
