Mehdi Ahmadi

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Mehdi Ahmadi, killed in Arak

On 18 Dey, Mehdi left home and was shot and killed by government forces during the protests in Arak. His family searched for him for four days before finally identifying his body.

Mehdi lived in the village of Kazaz near Arak.
