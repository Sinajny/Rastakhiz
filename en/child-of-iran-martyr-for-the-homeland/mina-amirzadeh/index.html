Mina Amirzadeh

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Mina Amirzadeh, 25, killed in Tehran

On 19 Dey, Mina was with her father in Tehran when government forces shot her in the neck. She died in her father's arms.

Mina was a microbiology student at Kavian University in Mashhad.
