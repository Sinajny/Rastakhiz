Nazanin-Zahra Esmikhani

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Nazanin-Zahra Esmikhani, 17, killed in Qazvin

On 18 Dey, during the protests in Qazvin, government forces shot Nazanin in the forehead, killing her. Her family received her body two days later.

Nazanin was a photographer and played the guitar.

She was buried in the village of Hesar Khorvan.

Dear Nazanin, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
