Hajer Eshaghi

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Hajer Eshaghi, 42, killed in Shahrak-e Keshvari, Isfahan

On 19 Dey, in the Shahrak-e Keshvari neighborhood of Isfahan, government forces shot Hajer in the heart with live ammunition, killing her.

Hajer was a commercial department employee. She had lost her husband years earlier and lived with her child.

Her body was returned to her family several days later, and she was buried in Bagh-e Rezvan cemetery in Isfahan on 26 Dey.
