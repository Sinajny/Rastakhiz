Saeed Asgharzadeh

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Saeed Asgharzadeh, 33, killed in Kamalshahr, Karaj

On 18 Dey, at around 10 p.m. in Kamalshahr, Karaj, government forces shot Saeed in the head with live ammunition. His friends took him toward Hashtgerd Hospital, but he died before reaching the hospital.

Dear Saeed, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
