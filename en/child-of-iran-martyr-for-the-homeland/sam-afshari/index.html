Sam Afshari

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Sam Afshari, 17, killed in Azimiyeh, Karaj

On 18 Dey, near Mehran Square in Azimiyeh, Karaj, government forces shot Sam in the back of the head with live ammunition from close range. He was still conscious when taken to Madani Hospital. According to the memorial account, while he was in hospital with a breathing tube, another shot was fired at him and he died.

Sam studied computing at school and was a provincial swimming champion.

His body was handed over to his family after they were charged 180 million tomans as a so-called “bullet fee.”

Dear Sam, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
