Mohsen Ebrahimzadeh

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Mohsen Ebrahimzadeh, 37, killed in Saveh

On 19 Dey, while helping wounded people near Saveh Road and Soltanabad in Baharestan County, Mohsen was shot by government forces and lost his life.

Mohsen owned a gym and was a bodybuilding coach.
