Mohsen Ebrahimzadeh

Child of Iran, Martyr for the Homeland
Mohsen Ebrahimzadeh, 37, died in the Saveh area.

On 19 Dey, while helping injured people near Saveh Road and Soltanabad in Baharestan County, Mohsen was shot and killed by government forces, according to the Persian memorial account.

Mohsen owned a gym and worked as a bodybuilding coach.
