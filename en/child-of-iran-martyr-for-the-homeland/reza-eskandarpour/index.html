Reza Eskandarpour

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Reza Eskandarpour, 37, killed in Aryashahr, Tehran

On 18 Dey, before setting out for the protests, Reza entrusted his phone and Instagram password to his mother. As he moved to help a wounded friend, government forces shot him from behind and killed him. At his farewell ceremony, his car was covered with flowers, and his fiancée came to his grave wearing a wedding dress.

Dear Reza, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
