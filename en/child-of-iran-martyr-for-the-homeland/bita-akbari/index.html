Bita Akbari

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Bita Akbari, 19, killed in Isfahan

On 19 Dey, government forces shot Bita in the head with live ammunition during the protests in Isfahan, killing her. Her injuries were so severe that her mother identified her by a tattoo on her hand.

Bita had recently become engaged.
