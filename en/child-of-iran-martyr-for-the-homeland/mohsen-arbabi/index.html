Mohsen Arbabi

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Mohsen Arbabi, 20, killed in Karaj

On 18 Dey, during the protests in Karaj, government forces shot Mohsen in the head with live ammunition, killing him. After a long search, his family found his body at Alborz Hospital.

Mohsen was 20 years old, Baluch and originally from Gosht in Saravan.

He was buried in his hometown on 23 Dey.
