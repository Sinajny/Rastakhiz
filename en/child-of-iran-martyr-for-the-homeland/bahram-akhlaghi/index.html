Bahram Akhlaghi

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Bahram Akhlaghi, 59, killed in Karaj

On 19 Dey, government forces fired pellets at Bahram from close range in Hashtgerd, Karaj, severely injuring his skull, face, heart and lungs. After a week in a coma, he died.

Bahram was a disabled veteran of the Iran–Iraq War, was married and had two children.
