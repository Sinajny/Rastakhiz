Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Jafar Amiri

Jafar Amiri, 31, killed in Zirab, Mazandaran

On 19 Dey, Jafar was standing at the edge of a square in Zirab when electricity in the area was cut off. After the blackout, government forces shot him from behind in the heart and killed him.

Jafar had found a job at the water and wastewater company only three months earlier. He had lost his father less than a year before and was supporting his mother and sister. He was known for his humor and kindness and loved football.

He was buried on 21 Dey.

Dear Jafar, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
