Majid Asgharnezhad

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Majid Asgharnezhad, 32, killed in Amol

On 18 Dey, Majid was shot by government forces during the protests in Amol and was taken to hospital with injuries. His condition was improving and he was expected to be discharged, but his family suddenly received news that he had died.

Majid was 32 years old.
