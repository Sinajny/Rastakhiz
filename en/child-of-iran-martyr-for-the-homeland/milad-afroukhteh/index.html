Milad Afrookhteh

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Milad Afrookhteh, 35, killed in Rasht

On 18 Dey, on Motahhari Street in Rasht, Milad shielded others with his own body to protect them from gunfire. Government forces shot him twice with live ammunition, killing him.

Milad was a football goalkeeper. After earning a coaching qualification, he became a youth football coach, training children and teenagers from the village of Jubaneh in Sangar. He also played volleyball.

He was buried at night at Jubaneh cemetery with only a limited number of family members present.

Dear Milad, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
