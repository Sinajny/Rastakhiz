Mona Esmi

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Mona Esmi, 38, killed in Rasht

On 18 Dey, during the protests in Rasht, Mona was shot directly from behind by government forces and lost her life.

Mona was married and the mother of one child.

She was buried at Bagh-e Rezvan cemetery in Rasht.

Dear Mona, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
