Meysam Asadi

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Meysam Asadi, 19, killed in Karaj

On 18 Dey, in the Enghelab Street and Lower Hesarak area of Karaj, a government-force sniper shot Meysam in the throat with live ammunition, killing him.

Meysam was originally from Mianeh and lived in Karaj.

He was buried in Behesht-e Ali cemetery in Eshtehard.

Dear Meysam, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
