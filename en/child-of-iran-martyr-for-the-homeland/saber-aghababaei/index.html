Saber Aghababaei

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Saber Aghababaei, 25, killed in Shahsavar

On the evening of 18 Dey, government forces shot Saber with live ammunition in Shahsavar. He died on 19 Dey.

Saber was a mechanic and came from Nashtarud.

Despite pressure to hold the funeral in silence, people attended his farewell. He was buried in the cemetery of Tuban village near Nashtarud.

Dear Saber, you gave your life for Iran's freedom, and from now on we will devote our lives...
