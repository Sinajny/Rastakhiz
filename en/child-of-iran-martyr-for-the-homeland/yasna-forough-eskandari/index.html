Yasna (Forough) Eskandari

Child of Iran, Devoted to the Homeland

Yasna (Forough) Eskandari, killed on Kashani Boulevard, Tehran

On 18 Dey, Yasna was shot from behind by government forces with live ammunition in the Kashani Boulevard and Aryashahr area of Tehran. The bullet struck her lower back and she died.

Yasna was a lawyer, an artist and a charcoal portrait painter.

She was buried at Behesht-e Zahra Cemetery in Tehran.
