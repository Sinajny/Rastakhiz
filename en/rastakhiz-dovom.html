Rastakhiz II of the Iranian Nation ☀️

The First Rastakhiz

In Esfand 1353 (March 1975), the Rastakhiz Party was established by order of the King of Iran, Mohammad Reza Shah Pahlavi.

The aim of establishing the party was to unify Iran’s political system, with an emphasis on the Constitution, the modernization of Iran, and loyalty to the monarchy and to Iran.

One criticism of the party was that it had not emerged from an independent political and social movement. The party was eventually dissolved in Mehr 1357 (October 1978), a few months before the Islamic Revolution.

Yet perhaps, at the time, people did not fully understand the necessity of this unity and national solidarity.

The Second Rastakhiz

Years passed; people witnessed the Islamic Revolution and its destructive effects on Iran.

And today, a generation that had never seen the Shah has come to love him. They admire him, and when his son, Prince Reza Pahlavi, calls for Iran’s freedom, millions of Iranians who love their homeland take to the streets, and on 18 and 19 Dey, widespread and bloody repression takes place.

Yes, this is how the second Rastakhiz of the Iranian nation began…

This time, not by political decree, but from among the people themselves, brought about by a generation that had never seen the Shah, yet came to love him.

We carry forward the path of the young people and heroes who gave their lives for Iran on 18 and 19 Dey, and we honor their memory.

We carry forward the path of that young man, covered in blood, who, with his final breaths, wrote in his own blood:

“Long live the Shah”
